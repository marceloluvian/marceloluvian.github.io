Lumbi

Turn off, dim or turn on your Xbox controller's button light, on Windows and on Mac.

Download

Windows 11

On the Microsoft Store. Free.

Coming soon

Mac with Apple Silicon

macOS 26 or newer. Free. Updates itself.

Download for Mac

On a Mac: open the downloaded file and drag Lumbi to Applications.

What it does

Pick how you want the light with one click:

Off
Dim
Normal
Max
  • Remembers your setting. The controller doesn't keep the light you chose; Lumbi puts it back every time you connect it.
  • Always at hand. In the Windows tray or the Mac menu bar: one click on its icon and you're done; it stays out of your way while you play.
  • Looks like your system. On Windows 11 it follows your theme and accent color; on the Mac it uses macOS glass.
  • On the Mac, the controller's battery. With the controller over Bluetooth it shows its battery and tells you when it runs low.
  • In English and Spanish.

What it works with

  • Windows 11, or a Mac with Apple Silicon and macOS 26 or newer.
  • Current Xbox controllers connected with a USB cable.
  • On Windows it should work the same with the Xbox Wireless Adapter; that's still being tested.

Over Bluetooth the controller doesn't allow changing the light. Connect it with a cable and Lumbi applies your setting. On the Mac, over Bluetooth it shows the battery.

Your privacy

Lumbi doesn't collect or send any data. It only saves your setting on your computer. On the Mac it checks once a day for a new version, without sending any of your data, and reads the controller's battery only if you give it the Bluetooth permission. More in the privacy policy.
