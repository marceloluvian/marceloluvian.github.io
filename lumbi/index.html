Lumbi

Turn off or dim the light on your Xbox controller's button from the Windows tray.

Coming soon

What it does

Pick how you want the light with one click:

Off
Dim
Normal
Max
  • Remembers your setting. The controller doesn't keep the light you chose; Lumbi puts it back every time you connect it.
  • Lives in the tray. One click on its icon and you're done; it stays out of your way while you play.
  • Looks like Windows 11. It follows your light or dark theme and your accent color.
  • In English and Spanish.

What it works with

  • Windows 11.
  • Current Xbox controllers connected with a USB cable.
  • It should work the same with the Xbox Wireless Adapter for Windows; that's still being tested.

Over Bluetooth the controller doesn't allow changing the light. Connect it with a cable and Lumbi applies your setting.

Your privacy

Lumbi doesn't collect or send any data and doesn't connect to the internet. It only saves your setting on your PC. More in the privacy policy.
