Support

Something not working, or have an idea? Write to us.

luviandev@outlook.com

To help you faster, include

  • Which app you use and its version (see Settings → About).
  • Your Windows or macOS version.
  • What happened and what you expected. A screenshot helps a lot.

We reply as soon as we can, usually within a few days.

Lumbi: before you write

  • The light doesn't change and the controller is on Bluetooth: on that connection the controller doesn't allow changing the light. Connect it with a cable.
  • The normal light comes back when I connect the controller: check that "Remember my setting" is turned on.
  • I can't see the Lumbi icon: on Windows look under the hidden icons arrow on the taskbar, or open Lumbi from the Start menu. On a Mac it's in the menu bar, top right.
  • On a Mac I don't see the controller's battery: the battery shows with the controller connected by Bluetooth. Give Lumbi the Bluetooth permission with the "Show battery" button or in Settings > Battery.
