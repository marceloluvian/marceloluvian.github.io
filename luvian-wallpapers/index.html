Luvian Wallpapers

Animated wallpapers for your Mac desktop, with a character that follows your cursor.

Download for Mac

Free

What it does

  • Two wallpapers included. Christmas Patch, with three scenes (Posada, City and Living room), and Halloween.
  • One for each display. Choose a wallpaper and its sound for each screen.
  • Make it yours. Change the color of the character and its stripe.
  • Takes care of your battery. It pauses when an app is full screen, the screen sleeps or the Mac is locked, and it slows down on battery or if the Mac gets warm.
  • More wallpapers. Open .lwp wallpapers shared by other people with a double click.
  • Updates itself. It lets you know when there's a new version.
  • In English and Spanish.

What it works with

  • macOS 13 Ventura or later.
  • Macs with Apple silicon (M1 or later).

To install it, open the file you downloaded and drag Luvian Wallpapers to Applications.

Your privacy

Luvian Wallpapers doesn't collect or send any data. It only connects to the internet to check for new versions. Your settings stay on your Mac, and wallpapers can't use the internet, your camera, your microphone or your location. More in the privacy policy.
