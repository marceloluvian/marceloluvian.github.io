Privacy policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026. Applies to all luviandev apps and to this site.

1. In short

luviandev apps don't collect or send your personal data. They work without accounts, ads or analytics.

2. What stays on your PC

Some apps save your preferences, such as language or settings, so they remember them next time. That stays on your PC, never leaves it and never reaches us. When you uninstall the app, that data is removed.

3. Access to features of your PC

Some apps need to use system features or devices connected to your PC to do their job. They only use what they need for that, and everything stays on your PC.

4. Internet and links

The apps don't send information over the internet. Luvian Wallpapers for Mac only connects to check for new versions: that check doesn't include your data, although the server that answers it, like any website, may log technical data such as the IP address. If you tap a link inside an app, for example to our social media, your browser opens, and that site has its own privacy rules.

5. If you write to us

When you write to luviandev@outlook.com, we use your email address and what you tell us only to reply. Messages stay in that mailbox as long as needed to help you, and we delete them if you ask. The mailbox is provided by an external email provider.

6. Digital stores

If you get an app from a digital store, that store handles your data under its own privacy policy. It only shares general statistics with us, such as how many people downloaded an app, with nothing that identifies you. If there is ever a purchase, the store takes care of the whole payment.

7. This site

This site uses no cookies, analytics or advertising. Like any website, the service that hosts it may log technical data about visits, such as the IP address, to keep it secure.

8. Who we share data with

What you send us by email isn't shared with anyone, unless the law requires it.

9. Children

Since the apps don't collect data, they don't collect data from children either.

10. Privacy notice

Data controller: Juan Marcelo Luvián (luviandev), in Mexico.

What data and why: the only personal data we may ever have is what you send us by email (your address and what you write), and it is used only to answer your message.

Your rights: you can ask us to show you that data, correct it or delete it, object to its use or withdraw your permission. Write to luviandev@outlook.com telling us what you want and which messages it is about. We reply within 20 business days at most, free of charge.

11. Changes

If this policy changes, we'll publish the new version here with its date.
